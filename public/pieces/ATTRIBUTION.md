# Chess piece artwork

This directory contains the Cburnett, Merida, and Alpha SVG piece sets sourced from the Lichess asset collection:
https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/tree/master/public/piece

- Cburnett — Colin M. L. Burnett; multi-licensed, including BSD-3-Clause.
- Merida — classic Merida chess font SVG adaptation; GPL-2.0-or-later.
- Alpha — Eric Bentzen; retained from the Lichess asset collection.

The source artwork and its applicable license terms remain with their respective authors.
